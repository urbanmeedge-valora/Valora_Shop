Good to know

How does ordering work?

Tap "Order" on any product, fill in your name, phone and delivery address, and choose cash on delivery or UPI. We'll message you on WhatsApp to confirm before it ships — no account or app download needed.

Is cash on delivery really available?

Yes, on every product shown here, across most serviceable pincodes in India. If COD isn't available at your pincode, we'll let you know when we confirm your order and offer UPI instead.

What if I want to return or exchange something?

You can request a return or size exchange within 7 days of delivery if the fit isn't right or the item arrives damaged. Message us with your order details and we'll sort out a pickup or exchange.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders ship within 2–4 business days and arrive within a week, depending on your location. You'll get delivery updates on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed.

Privacy Policy

What information we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, city, pincode, chosen payment method, and any notes you add. We don't ask for or store card details — UPI payments are made directly through your own payment app.

How we use it

Your details are used only to confirm, process and deliver your order, and to contact you about it on WhatsApp or by phone. We don't sell or rent your information to third parties, and we don't use it for marketing without your consent.

How it's stored

Order details are kept in a private record accessible only to Valora, and order confirmations are sent through WhatsApp. We don't use tracking cookies or third-party ad trackers on this page.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing us. We'll respond within a few days.

Questions about this policy

Orders typically ship within 2–4 business days and arrive within a week, depending on location and courier serviceability. Delivery timelines are estimates, not guarantees, and can vary during sales or due to courier delays.
